Vearl testnet

Free test tokens. To try the network, not to keep.

Paste the address of your testnet wallet and receive test tokens to pay fees and create jobs. They have no value, are not for sale and may disappear when the testnet is reset.

Create a wallet with vearl-wallet new: the address starts with vrl1. The faucet never asks for your keys or your 24 words.

One request per address and per visitor each hour, and a daily cap for everybody.

  2. Look at your balance in the explorer.
  3. Run a miner or a worker: see the miner guide in the project’s repository (docs/15).
