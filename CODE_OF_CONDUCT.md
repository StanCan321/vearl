# Code of conduct

We want Vearl to be a place where people can discuss hard technical questions without friction.

- Be respectful. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in any form.
- Keep discussions on topic. This is a testnet: no price talk, no financial advice, no promotion.
- Never share anybody's private information. Never ask for, or share, a seed phrase, a private key or a password.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who do so repeatedly.
To report a problem, write to contact@vearl.org.
